PG & HOSTEL MANAGEMENT SaaS

Market Research • Product Requirements • Subscription Model • Technical Roadmap

Prepared for Uyar Technologies Private Limited | 22 October 2026

Planning draft — prices and timelines are recommendations, not vendor quotations.

1. Executive Summary

Build a multi-tenant, subscription-based cloud application for men’s PGs, women’s PGs, student hostels, working-professional accommodation and co-living operators. Each PG owner signs up, starts a time-limited trial, sets up one or more properties, manages tenants and payments, and subscribes based on active bed capacity. Uyar operates a separate super-admin console.

Recommended positioning: Tamil/English-first, mobile-friendly PG operations software with transparent per-bed pricing, trustworthy rent ledgers, flexible electricity billing, and reliable WhatsApp/email reminders.

4. End-to-End Customer Journey

Owner visits marketing website → chooses Start Free Trial → verifies mobile/email via OTP.

Owner provides PG name, type, address, contact, number of properties, beds and preferred billing method.

System creates isolated PG workspace; guided wizard adds floors, rooms, beds, rent rules and electricity rates.

Owner imports tenants via Excel or adds one by one; sends self-onboarding links.

Trial runs for 14 days (suggested) with feature limits and clear countdown.

Owner selects subscription, pays through gateway, receives SaaS tax invoice and continues service.

Recurring renewals, invoices, reminders and reports run per configured schedule.

If unpaid: grace period → read-only access → suspension of new edits; preserve data and provide export per policy.

5. Functional Requirements — PG Owner Dashboard

5.1 Property, Room and Bed Inventory

Create multiple PG properties; building → floor → room → bed hierarchy.

Room types: single, double, triple, four-sharing and configurable.

Bed states: available, reserved, occupied, notice, maintenance; prevent double assignment.

Occupancy calendar, upcoming vacancies, room transfers, historical allocations.

5.2 Tenant Onboarding and KYC

Name, phone, emergency contact, occupation/college, move-in date, rent and deposit.

Collect consented ID proof and agreement securely; configurable document retention.

Guardian contact optional for students; upload agreement, photograph and documents.

Record check-in checklist, room/bed assignment and welcome message.

5.3 Rent and Deposits

Configurable due day: calendar month or anniversary cycle.

Recurring rent, prorated first/last month, advance payments, partial payments, discounts and late fees.

Separate refundable deposit ledger with deductions, refunds and approval trail.

UPI/cash/bank/card recording; payment status must not change to paid from screenshot alone.

5.4 Electricity and Utilities

Support fixed monthly charge, per-bed share, room sub-meter and tenant-specific meter.

Store previous/current readings, units, tariff slabs/fixed rate, common charges and adjustments.

Owner reviews draft bill before posting; lock issued bill and use credit notes for corrections.

Water, Wi-Fi, laundry, food and maintenance as configurable bill line items.

5.5 Billing and Invoices

Scheduled monthly invoice creation, unique invoice number, issue/due dates, itemized charges.

PDF invoice and payment receipt; tax treatment and GST fields configurable after accountant review.

Credit notes, adjustments, payment allocation, aging reports and downloadable statements.

5.6 Reminders and Messaging

Owner-defined reminder schedule: 5 days before, due date, 3 days after, etc.

WhatsApp Business templates (approved), email, optional SMS; delivery logs and retry controls.

Tenant opt-in/consent, quiet hours, suppression after payment and language templates.

Overdue escalation to owner/manager, without abusive or excessive messaging.

5.7 Operations

Complaints with priority, status, assignee, SLA and photos.

Visitor log, optional gate QR, notice board and emergency contacts.

Staff roles, attendance optional, expenses, vendors, maintenance and mess billing as add-ons.

5.8 Analytics

Total beds, occupied/vacant, occupancy %, billed, collected, outstanding, deposit liabilities.

Expense categories, monthly cash flow, owner income vs cost, property comparisons.

CSV/Excel exports, monthly summaries and audit-friendly transaction history.

6. Uyar Super Admin and Subscription Engine

Manage plan catalog, per-bed limits, trials, promo codes, annual discounts and invoices.

Track signups, active trials, trial-to-paid conversion, MRR, churn, failed payments and active beds.

Provision isolated tenants/workspaces; enforce plan entitlements server-side.

Support tickets, announcements, usage monitoring, impersonation only via logged consented support workflow.

Payment webhooks with idempotency, invoice generation, renewal retries and grace-period policies.

Feature flags, operational audit logs, backups and account closure/export requests.

8. Product Differentiation and Validation

Tamil + English UI, Tamil message templates, simple mobile-first workflows for wardens.

One-click Excel import with duplicate detection and guided migration from notebooks.

Flexible electricity engine with transparent split calculations and disputes history.

Owner-friendly collection workflow: UPI payment links, ledger, reconciliation and reminder suppression.

Women’s PG optional visitor approval and emergency workflows with privacy safeguards.

Trust: clear fees, no data lock-in, exports, onboarding support, fast support turnaround.

Interview at least 20 owners across 10–30, 30–100 and 100+ bed categories; ask for current tools, monthly collection issues, actual reminder costs, willingness to pay and preferred languages. Run 3–5 pilots for one complete billing cycle before public launch.

9. Technical Architecture

Recommended MVP: React + TypeScript + Tailwind web frontend (responsive PWA), Node.js/NestJS or FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL, Redis-backed job queue, object storage (S3-compatible), payment gateway, transactional email and approved WhatsApp Business API provider. Deploy using Docker on AWS with managed database, TLS, monitoring and automated backups.

Data isolation: each business table carries tenant_id/property_id; enforce tenant-scoped queries and role permissions in backend, with tests against cross-tenant access. Start with modular monolith; split services only when justified by scale.

Core Data Model

organizations, users, memberships, properties, floors, rooms, beds, tenants, stays, documents, rent_rules, utility_meters, readings, charges, invoices, invoice_lines, payments, allocations, deposits, refunds, expenses, message_templates, message_jobs, tickets, subscriptions, plans, audit_logs.

Critical Non-Functional Requirements

Encryption in transit and at rest; secrets manager; MFA for owners/admins; least-privilege access.

Daily backups and restore tests; defined retention and deletion procedures.

Webhook signature validation, idempotent billing jobs, timezone-aware due dates and retry-safe scheduling.

Consent, privacy notice and purpose-limited KYC storage; review India DPDP obligations with counsel.

Accessible UI, mobile performance, logs, alerts, error tracking and rate limits.

10. Workflows and Business Rules

Tenant admission

Enquiry → application → document collection → owner approval → bed reservation → deposit recorded → agreement → check-in → active tenant → welcome message.

Monthly billing

Scheduled job → determine active stay and rent cycle → calculate rent and approved utilities → generate draft → owner review where required → issue immutable invoice → deliver notification → accept/record payment → reconcile → receipt → overdue escalation.

Checkout

Notice → move-out date → prorated rent/utility settlement → inspection → deposit deduction approval → refund → bed becomes available → archive stay (do not erase accounting history).

SaaS trial and expiry

Signup → trial start → usage tracking → upgrade prompts → paid subscription → renewal → failed-payment retry → grace period → restricted mode → reactivation/export/closure.

12. Acceptance Criteria for MVP

Two different PG owners cannot access each other’s data even by editing API identifiers.

A bed cannot be simultaneously assigned to two active stays.

One tenant can be billed for rent + measured electricity + fixed services with accurate totals.

Partial payments and deposits are represented separately and balance is correct.

Automated reminder is not sent after a payment clears; retry does not duplicate invoices.

Owner can download invoice, receipt, outstanding report and full tenant export.

Trial expiry, successful renewal and failed renewal behave as configured.

Backups can be restored and audit trail shows sensitive financial edits.

14. Launch Plan and KPIs

Pilot 3–5 properties → collect feedback → publish pricing → launch self-service trial → partner with PG associations/college-adjacent operators → scale through demos, referral credits and onboarding support.

KPIs: trial signup-to-setup rate, first tenant added, first invoice issued, trial-to-paid conversion, MRR, net revenue retention, support tickets/property, monthly active owners, reminder delivery success and overdue collection time.

15. Recommended Next Decisions

Confirm target: independent PG owners first, rather than college hostel ERP.

Choose pricing: bed tiers vs per-bed; validate in customer interviews.

Define electricity policy and tax invoice requirements with pilot owners/accountant.

Build clickable UI prototype and full product backlog before engineering.

Prioritize web/PWA MVP; build native apps only if pilot usage justifies them.
